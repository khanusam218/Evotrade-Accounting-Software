Internal Reference

Evotrade Accounting Software — Feature Register

A working list of everything the system does today, and a closer look at what was built or repaired in the most recent round of work.

Prepared 30 Jul 2026 Covers Sales · Purchases · Inventory · Manufacturing · POS · CRM · HR · Finance · Reports

What's New

§ 1

Newly built or newly working features, in the order they came up. Each entry: why it exists, what it gets you, and where to find it.

Add Call button (CRM)

new
Purpose

The Call Records screen had no way to actually log a call — the form existed, but nothing opened it.

Benefit

Staff can record a customer call the moment it happens instead of it going untracked.

How to use

CRM → Call → click + ADD CALL, fill in subject/contact/outcome, save.

Readable quantity, price & discount fields

fixed
Purpose

Those columns were so narrow that typed numbers were clipped and unreadable across most sales and purchase forms.

Benefit

You can actually see what you typed before saving — fewer mis-keyed quantities and prices.

How to use

Automatic. Applies to Quotations, Orders, Invoices, Returns, Receipts, Stock Adjustments, Recurring & Import Invoices.

Whole-number quantities

fixed
Purpose

Quantity fields were showing values like 5.0000 for a business that only ever sells whole units.

Benefit

Clean, whole quantities everywhere — nothing to mentally round off.

How to use

Automatic on every line-item quantity field, on entry and when re-opening a saved record.

Invoice ⇄ Delivery linking

fixed
Purpose

The "Invoice Deliveries" popup couldn't find a delivery unless it happened to share an order reference — most deliveries didn't.

Benefit

You can now see what's actually been delivered against an invoice, reliably.

How to use

Sales Invoices → row action → Invoice Deliveries.

Purchase Order → Purchase Invoice

new
Purpose

There was no way to bill a vendor directly from a confirmed order — everything had to be re-typed on a fresh invoice.

Benefit

One click carries the vendor, lines, and discount over. Faster billing, fewer transcription errors.

How to use

Purchase Orders → confirmed/received order → Invoice button.

Tax on Purchase Invoice, Purchase Return & Import Invoice

new
Purpose

These three forms had no tax selector at all, so purchase-side tax always came out as 0.00.

Benefit

Purchase and import tax totals are now real numbers, not placeholders.

How to use

Pick a rate from the new Tax column on any line item.

Assembly Jobs read the real BOM

fixed
Purpose

Starting a new Job Order always said "this BOM has no components" — it was reading from a list that never carried them.

Benefit

Job orders now pre-fill with the product's actual recipe, scaled to the quantity you're building.

How to use

Manufacturing → new Job Order → choose a product with a saved BOM.

Two independent carts at the register

new
Purpose

Invoice 1 / Invoice 2 tabs existed but shared one cart — switching tabs didn't actually switch anything.

Benefit

A cashier can park one customer's sale, help someone else on the second tab, then come back without losing either cart.

How to use

POS Terminal → click between Invoice 1 / Invoice 2 freely.

Sale Return, for real

new
Purpose

The Sale Return tab was a label with no logic behind it — it couldn't look anything up or return anything.

Benefit

Look up a past sale, return exactly what came back (capped at what's actually left to return), stock goes back into inventory automatically.

How to use

POS Terminal → Sale Return → search the invoice number → enter return quantities → Process Return.

See the invoice number, and browse past sales

new
Purpose

After checkout there was no way to know what number a sale got, and nowhere in the app listed individual POS sales.

Benefit

A confirmation shows the invoice number right after checkout; a new page lets you search/filter every past sale.

How to use

Banner appears after Confirm & Checkout. Full list at POS → Transactions.

POS sales post to the ledger

fixed
Purpose

Counter sales never touched the accounting books — Trial Balance and Profit & Loss were silently missing all POS revenue.

Benefit

Every sale, void, and return now posts a proper balanced journal entry, so your financial reports finally include register sales.

How to use

Automatic — happens the instant a sale is completed, voided, or returned.

Also tightened, quietly
  • Document numbering (invoices, orders, receipts, and ~30 other types) made collision-proof, so imported or older records can no longer clash with new ones.
  • Error messages now say what actually went wrong ("this already exists", "a required field is missing") instead of a generic failure message.
